# Brand Battle

Brand Battle is a competition where talented designers compete against each other by scoring the highest on brand identity & logo design. It was originally conceptualized by @BurritoDesigns in 2020, which led to the creation of the first Brand Battle. It also has a spin-off called Mini Battle, created by @ManageLimit in 2026 as a shorter version of the main competition.
